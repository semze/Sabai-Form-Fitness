About Sabai Form

Sabai Form Fitness was created to give women a place where they can move, grow stronger, and feel confident. without judgment and without pressure.

Led by Sannah, a mom and certified coach who understands the unique demands on a woman’s body. Classes blend strength, mobility, and core work in a supportive environment that meets you exactly where you are.
